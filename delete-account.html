Delete your Umbra Player account

Umbra Player is an app by EchoDisc. This page explains how to delete an Umbra Player account and the data stored with it. You can ask for deletion without installing the app.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Umbra Player and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  4. Read the message and tap Delete forever.

Your account and its data are deleted from our server straight away, and the app signs you out.

Or ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, write to business@echodisc.com from the email address your account uses, with the subject Delete my Umbra Player account. We delete the account and all its data within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your account, your email address and your sign-in.
  • Your household profiles, their names and avatars, and the kids PIN.
  • Your saved playlist and server settings (stored encrypted).
  • Favorites and favorite folders, collections, watch progress and watch history.
  • Hidden groups, interface preferences, and any TV-pairing package.

All of it is deleted at the same moment. We keep no archive copy.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Attempt records. If you ever typed a wrong invite code or paired a TV, a record of that attempt (a random account number and the time, without your email) can stay on our server. After deletion it no longer links to anyone.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider keeps routine request logs for about one day, then deletes them.
  • Artwork cache. Posters and descriptions of films and series are kept in a shared cache. It never recorded who looked them up, so it holds nothing about you.

What stays on your devices

Deleting the account does not remove the app or its data on your phone, tablet or TV. Playlists you added on a device stay there until you remove them or uninstall Umbra Player, which deletes everything it stored on that device.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
